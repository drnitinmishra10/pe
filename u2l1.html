✍️ What is a Prompt?

Definition:
A Prompt is the specific text instruction or query provided to an AI model (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) to guide its response generation.

Simple Definition

A prompt is the bridge of communication between human intent and machine understanding.

Key Characteristics of Effective Prompts

  • Clarity and Precision
  • Contextual Relevance
  • Structured Format
  • Explicit Expectations

⚡ Casual Queries vs Structured Prompts

Parameter Casual Query Structured Prompt
Clarity Vague & Informal Specific & Intentional
Context Minimal or Missing Rich Context & Background Included
Output Quality Generic & Variable Tailored & Highly Precise
Example "Write about AI." "Act as a computer science professor. Write a 200-word introduction to AI for first-year engineering students."

The Core Difference

  • Casual Queries: High ambiguity → Generic or irrelevant responses
  • Structured Prompts: High specificity → Accurate and actionable responses

💡 Why is Prompt Design Important?

  • Reduces Model Hallucinations and inaccuracies
  • Saves time by minimizing iterative revisions
  • Ensures consistent tone, style, and formatting
  • Unlocks hidden capabilities of Large Language Models

🎓 Classroom Activity

  1. Take a simple prompt: "Explain sorting algorithms."
  2. Rewrite it by adding a target audience, word limit, and tone requirement.
  3. Compare the outputs generated by both prompts in an LLM tool.

📝 Assignment

  1. Draft three different prompts targeting the same topic (e.g., "Cloud Computing") for three distinct audiences: a middle school student, a college student, and an IT professional.
  2. Document the differences in output obtained from an LLM for each.

📌 Lecture Summary

  • A prompt directs the behavior and output of Large Language Models.
  • The clarity of input directly governs the quality of AI response.
  • Structured prompts yield vastly superior results compared to casual search queries.
  • Prompt design is an essential foundational skill for modern software and engineering workflows.
